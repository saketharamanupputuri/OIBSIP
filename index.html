A LIFE THAT INSPIRED MILLIONS

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Scientist, teacher and the People's President of India

About his life

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam (1931–2015) was an Indian aerospace scientist who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. He was widely admired for his humility and his encouragement of young people to learn, imagine and contribute to society.

Important milestones

  • 1931Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.
  • 1960sBegan working with India's space and defence research programmes.
  • 1980India's SLV-III successfully placed the Rohini satellite into orbit.
  • 2002–2007Served as the President of India and connected with students across the country.
  • 2015Passed away while delivering a lecture to students in Shillong.

His legacy reminds us that curiosity, education and public service can help shape a better future.

Why he is remembered

Kalam continues to inspire students through his scientific work, books and belief in the power of education. This page is a small student tribute to his contributions.
